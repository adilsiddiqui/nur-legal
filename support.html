NUR — Support

Something not working, or a question about how NUR behaves? Email buildwithnuha@gmail.com and you will get a reply, usually within two to three days.

It helps a great deal if you include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you expected to happen. There is no account and no support ticket number — just write.

NUR is free. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no ads, so there is nothing to cancel, restore or refund. If an app is charging you for NUR, it is not this app.

Common questions

My prayer times look wrong

Prayer times depend on three things, all of which you control:

  • Location. NUR needs location access to calculate times for where you actually are. If you denied it, prayer times cannot be computed. Turn it on in Settings → NUR → Location.
  • Calculation method. Different authorities use different sun angles for Fajr and Isha. Open the gear icon on the home screen and choose the method your local mosque follows. This is the usual cause of a time being off by 10–20 minutes.
  • Madhab. Asr falls at a different time in the Hanafi school than in the others. Set it alongside the calculation method.

If the Hijri date is a day out from your local moon sighting, there is a Hijri adjustment setting to shift it forward or back.

The Fajr alarm did not go off

  • NUR needs permission to schedule alarms. Check Settings → NUR and make sure alarm access is allowed.
  • Confirm the alarm is switched on, and check the wake offset — the alarm fires relative to Fajr, so if your Fajr time changed, so did the alarm.
  • The alarm rings at the media volume, not the ringer volume. If your media volume is at zero, turn it up before sleeping.
  • Focus and Do Not Disturb do not silence it, but a powered-off phone does.

I cannot dismiss the alarm

That is deliberate. The alarm asks you to complete a short challenge first — either typing out a morning du'a transliteration, or walking a set number of steps. You can change which challenge you get, or how many steps, in the alarm settings. You can also turn the alarm off entirely.

Salah Shield is not blocking anything

  • Shield needs Screen Time permission. If you declined it, grant it in Settings → Screen Time and then re-open Shield in NUR.
  • You must pick which apps to block — NUR never chooses for you.
  • Blocking runs in a window around each prayer. Check how many minutes before and after the adhan you set; outside that window nothing is blocked.
  • Marking the prayer as prayed lifts the block early.

NUR cannot see which apps you chose, and nothing about your app usage leaves your device. The blocking is done by iOS itself.

How accurate is the Halal Scanner?

Treat it as a research aid, not a ruling. Scanning a barcode looks the product up in the Open Food Facts database; photographing an ingredient list reads the text on your device and checks each ingredient against a local reference. Both can be wrong — a database entry may be out of date, an ingredient may be ambiguous about its source, and the same E-number can be plant or animal derived depending on the manufacturer.

NUR flags what deserves a second look. Where a ruling matters, verify with the manufacturer or ask a scholar.

My widget is showing old information

Widgets update on their own schedule, which iOS controls. If a widget says to open NUR, open the app once and it will refresh. This also happens after you change your location or calculation settings, because the widget recomputes from the new values.

I am not getting notifications

Check that notifications are allowed in Settings → NUR → Notifications, then check inside NUR that the reminders you want are switched on — the per-prayer reminders and the daily reminders are separate toggles.

Does NUR work without internet?

Yes, almost entirely. Prayer times, qibla, the alarm, the whole library and the ingredient reference all work offline. Only the barcode lookup in the Halal Scanner and the weather reading need a connection.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything NUR stores — your prayer logs, reflections, settings and reading progress — is kept on your device only, so removing the app removes all of it. There is no account to close and no server holding a copy. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Which devices does NUR support?

iPhone running iOS 26.2 or later. NUR is an iPhone app; it is not designed for iPad.

Reporting a content correction

NUR carries Qur'an, hadith, du'a and guide content, every item with its reference. If you find a translation, a reference or an authenticity grading that is wrong, please write in — tell us which screen it appeared on and what you believe the correct reading is. Corrections to religious content are taken seriously and handled before feature work.
