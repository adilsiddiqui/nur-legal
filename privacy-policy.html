Privacy Policy for NUR

Last updated: 27 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the NUR mobile application (“NUR”, “the app”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use it. NUR is published by Sadaf Mohammad Naeim Siddiqui (“the Publisher”), who is the data fiduciary/controller responsible for the app.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at buildwithnuha@gmail.com.

1. Summary (the short version)

NUR is designed to be private by default:

  • There is no account, login, sign-up, name, or email collection. You can use NUR completely anonymously.
  • We do not run any analytics, advertising, or tracking. NUR contains no advertising SDKs and does not access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your location stays on your device. It is used only to calculate prayer times and qibla direction. NUR never sends your location to our servers (we don’t operate any).
  • The only data that leaves your device is (a) a product barcode or product name you scan in the Halal Scanner, which is sent to third-party food databases to look up ingredients, and (b) subscription/purchase information handled by Apple and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, if you choose to subscribe.

The sections below explain this in detail.

2. Information we do not collect

NUR does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit to us:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials (there are no accounts).
  • Your contacts, photos library, calendar, or health data.
  • Any advertising identifier (IDFA) or cross-app tracking data.
  • Analytics or usage-behaviour data of any kind.

We do not operate servers that receive your personal information.

3. Information processed on your device

Some features require your device to process information locally. This information is not sent to us:

  • Location (prayer times & qibla). With your permission, NUR reads your location to calculate accurate prayer times and the direction of the qibla. Your coordinates are stored only on your device and are used on-device for these calculations. To show your city name, NUR uses Apple’s built-in location/geocoding service; this is a standard Apple system service governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Camera & ingredient photos (Halal Scanner). When you scan an ingredient label, the image is processed entirely on your device using Apple’s on-device text-recognition (Vision) technology. Photos of labels are not uploaded anywhere and are not retained after processing.
  • Motion & step count (wake-up challenge). If you use the step-based wake-up challenge, NUR reads your device’s motion/step data on-device to verify you have completed the challenge. This data is not transmitted off your device.
  • Your activity within the app (e.g. prayers you mark, reflections you write, reading progress) is stored locally on your device using Apple’s on-device database. It is not sent to us.

4. Information shared with third parties for app functionality

NUR uses a small number of third-party services strictly to make specific features work. We share only the minimum data needed, and never your identity or location with these services.

a) Open Food Facts (Halal Scanner — barcodes)

When you scan a product barcode, NUR sends that barcode to Open Food Facts to retrieve the product’s ingredient and category information. Only the barcode is sent — no personal identifiers and no location. See the Open Food Facts privacy notice.

b) USDA FoodData Central (Halal Scanner — ingredient lookup)

If a product is not found above, NUR may send the product name to the U.S. Department of Agriculture’s FoodData Central API to look up ingredient information. Only the product name is sent — no personal identifiers and no location. See USDA / api.data.gov.

c) Apple (system services, notifications, purchases)

  • NUR schedules local prayer reminders and alarms on your device. These do not involve any remote server and no data is sent to us.
  • If you subscribe, your purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

d) RevenueCat (subscription management)

If you purchase a subscription, we use RevenueCat, Inc. to validate and manage your subscription status. RevenueCat receives your purchase/transaction information and an app-generated anonymous identifier. This information is used only to determine whether your subscription is active. It is not linked to your name or used for advertising or tracking. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

We do not sell or rent any information to anyone.

5. Permissions NUR may request

You are always in control. NUR requests these permissions only when a feature needs them, and the app continues to work (with that feature limited) if you decline:

Permission Why NUR asks
Location Calculate accurate prayer times and qibla direction for your area.
Camera Scan product barcodes and ingredient labels in the Halal Scanner.
Motion & Fitness Count steps for the optional step-based Fajr wake-up challenge.
Notifications Show prayer-time reminders and other reminders you enable.
Alarms Wake you for Fajr with the wake-up alarm.

You can change any of these at any time in your device’s Settings → NUR.

6. Data storage, retention, and backups

  • App data (your settings, prayer logs, reflections, reading progress) is stored locally on your device. Because it lives on your device, it is removed when you delete the app.
  • Your device’s own backup system (for example, iCloud or encrypted device backups) may include NUR’s local data as part of your overall device backup. Those backups are controlled by you and governed by Apple’s terms, not by us.
  • We do not retain your personal data on our servers because we do not collect it there.

7. Children’s privacy

NUR is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because NUR does not collect personal information through an account in the first place, no special children’s data is gathered.

8. Your rights

Because NUR is privacy-first and does not hold your personal data on our servers, you already control your information directly: it lives on your device, and deleting the app removes it.

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and other applicable data-protection laws, you have rights that may include:

  • the right to access information about how your personal data is processed;
  • the right to correction and erasure of your personal data;
  • the right to grievance redressal; and
  • the right to nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any right, or to raise a grievance, contact us at buildwithnuha@gmail.com and we will respond within the timelines required by applicable law. For data held by third-party services described in Section 4, please also refer to those services’ own privacy policies.

9. Security

We rely on Apple’s platform security and on transmitting third-party requests (Section 4) over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, but because NUR keeps your information on your own device and collects no personal data on our servers, your exposure is minimal.

10. International users

NUR is operated from India. The third-party services in Section 4 may process the limited data described there on servers located in other countries (for example, the United States). Where data is transferred internationally, it is limited to the non-identifying, functional data described above.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app. Your continued use of NUR after an update means you accept the revised policy.
